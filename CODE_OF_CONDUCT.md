# Code of Conduct

RevScope adopts the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We are committed to a harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer via GitHub: [@santiquiroz](https://github.com/santiquiroz).

The maintainer will review and respond to reports as promptly as possible. All complaints will be investigated and handled as appropriate to the circumstances.
